Privacy policy

Story Bot Studio · last updated 12 October 2026

Story Bot Studio is an automation tool run by its operator for YouTube channels they manage. It writes and renders videos and uploads them to a YouTube channel whose owner has signed in with Google and allowed it to.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time on the connected channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the connected channel's name, so the operator can confirm videos go to the right channel.
  • Email address (openid, userinfo.email): to show the operator which Google account a channel is connected with.

The app does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, playlists, subscribers, messages or any other Google data, and never sees the account password.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token, channel name and email are stored privately by the operator (on the operator's computer and in an encrypted secret of the automation that runs the daily uploads) and used only to upload that channel's videos.
  • The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Videos are uploaded through the YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or from the YouTube channel's security settings. After that, no further uploads are possible and the stored token stops working; ask the operator to delete it as well.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
